# Code of conduct

1. **No blame, just solutions.** Argue about ideas, not people or sides.
2. **Assume good faith**, and act in good faith.
3. **Be honest.** Cite what you claim, and say when something is a guess or written by AI.
4. **Protect people.** No private information and no targeting anyone.
5. **Remember the people behind the complaints.** Someone saying "we're drowning" meant it.

Breaking these leads to content removal and, for repeat or serious cases, a block. Details are in
[MODERATION.md](MODERATION.md).
