Why this exists

The people who are hurting are the ones who stock the shelves, drive the trucks, care for the sick and keep the lights on. Many of them helped build other people's fortunes. Town Hall puts what they're living through in one place, in their own words, next to fixes that are researched and sourced.

Every researched fix says who can make it happen: government, people and companies with means (this is their country too), and you, this week. Back the ones you believe in. The counts are the message.

Make it better

Who should do it?

Town Hall is what needs fixing. Its sister project, The Balanced Ticket, is who should lead: a public vote on an independent, merit-picked team.

The Balanced Ticket

An independent, open-source citizens' project. Posts are screened by AI for spam, private details and personal attacks, never for politics. Voices from X and Bluesky are public posts with names and handles removed. Researched fixes are written with sources and reviewed in the open on GitHub; fixes marked AI draft were written by AI from what people said here.
