Why this exists

The people who are hurting are the ones who stock the shelves, drive the trucks, care for the sick and keep the lights on. Many of them helped build other people's fortunes. Town Hall puts what they're living through in one place, in their own words, next to fixes that are researched and sourced.

Back the fixes you believe in and share them. The counts are the message, to everyone who can do something about it.
